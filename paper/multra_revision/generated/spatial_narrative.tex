In the fresh matched cohort, 12 LLM outcomes, 14 rule outcomes, and 12 random outcomes contain at least one edge outside the monitored neighborhood with more than 60~s additional accumulated time loss. For the LLM, the median case-level maximum remote-edge increase is 1272.36~s (including no-ops), and the largest is 5685.33~s. The largest LLM remote 500~m-cell net increase is 5091.84~s. These increases coexist with satisfaction of the default global and one-hop constraints. Complete summary and worst-edge records for both historical and fresh cohorts accompany the paper.
